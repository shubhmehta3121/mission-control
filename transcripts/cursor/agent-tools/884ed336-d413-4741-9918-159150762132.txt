# MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members (June 2018)

## Study Guide

All Easy Hard Not Studied

What does the designation "qualified in submarines" represent for enlisted members?

An intensive personal effort to meet the high standards and special requirements of submarine duty

#### Learn More

The designation "qualified in submarines" represents an intensive personal effort to meet the high standards and special requirements of submarine duty. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 1)

Who is typically assigned to submarine training?

Volunteers, with nonvolunteers assigned only when sufficient volunteers cannot be obtained

#### Learn More

Only volunteers will be assigned to submarine training under normal circumstances. Nonvolunteers will be assigned only when sufficient volunteers cannot be obtained. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 1)

Is assignment to submarine duty elective after issuance of a transfer directive?

No, it is not elective

#### Learn More

Based on the investment of training costs and long planning time, assignment to submarine duty is not elective after issuance of a transfer directive leading to duty in submarines. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 1)

Where are the eligibility requirements for submarine duty found?

#### Learn More

The specified eligibility requirements for submarine duty may be found in MILPERSMAN 1306-402. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 1)

What is the prerequisite training for enlisted members for submarine duty, except for nuclear trained personnel?

Basic Enlisted Submarine School at Naval Submarine Base, New London, Groton, CT

Basic Enlisted Submarine School is the basic training of enlisted members for submarine duty at Naval Submarine Base, New London, Groton, CT is a prerequisite for qualification in submarines for all enlisted ratings, with the exception of nuclear trained personnel. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 1)

Are enlisted personnel from Basic Enlisted Submarine School considered "qualified in submarines" upon arrival at a submarine?

No, they are not considered qualified nor is their training complete

Commanding officers (COs) of submarines receiving enlisted personnel from this school must not consider them "qualified in submarines," nor consider their training complete. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 1)

What must enlisted personnel pass to be designated "qualified in submarines"?

An oral and practical examination demonstrating sufficient knowledge of ship's systems and operating procedures

Before being designated "qualified in submarines," each individual must pass an oral and practical examination that demonstrates sufficient knowledge of ship's systems and operating procedures pertaining to the submarine in which serving. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 2)

How long must personnel serve aboard a submarine before being eligible for "qualified in submarines" designation?

Personnel are not eligible to be designated "qualified in submarines" by the CO until they have served aboard a submarine for 6 months. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 2)

What must the commanding officer ensure for personnel to validate the "qualified in submarines" designation?

Sufficient underway time

#### Learn More

The CO must ensure personnel have sufficient underway time to ensure the validity of the designation "qualified in submarines." (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 2)

Who issues amplifying details for submarine qualification?

Submarine force commanders

Amplifying details for qualification are issued by the submarine force commanders. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 2)

Who can request a waiver of eligibility standards for personnel in non-submarine source ratings completing submarine qualification requirements during temporary duty?

The personnel, with endorsement by the submarine CO

#### Learn More

Personnel serving in source ratings other than submarines who complete requirements for qualification in submarines while serving temporary additional duty on board a submarine may request a waiver of eligibility standards. The waiver request must be addressed to Navy Personnel Command (NAVPERSCOM), Nuclear Power/Submarine Distribution Branch (PERS-403) and endorsed by the submarine CO; a copy must be provided to the submarine force commander. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 2)

What designation is assigned if NAVPERSCOM approves a waiver for non-submarine source rating personnel qualified in submarines?

Designation 7 (SG)

If approved by NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403), submarine "designation 7 (SG)" will be assigned. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 2)

What physical standards must enlisted members meet before submarine assignment?

Standards specified in NAVMED P-117, Manual of the Medical Department

Before being assigned to a submarine, each enlisted member must meet the physical standards specified in reference (a). (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 2)

What does Designation 1 (SS) indicate?

Qualified in submarines

What does Designation 2 (SU) indicate?

In training for submarine qualifications

#### Learn More

Designated "in training for submarine qualifications." (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 2)

What does Designation 5 (SQ) indicate?

Qualified for submarines, not planned for future submarine duty

#### Learn More

Designated "qualified for submarines." Not planned for future assignment to submarine duty. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 2)

What does Designation 7 (SG) indicate?

Qualified for submarines, not in submarine source rating or NEC, not detailed by submarine community

#### Learn More

Designated "qualified for submarines." Not in submarine source rating or Navy enlisted classification (NEC). Not detailed by the submarine community. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 2)

What does Designation 8 (SP) indicate?

Previously assigned in submarines, disqualified, assigned to submarine support billet, not reassigned to submarine duty without NAVPERSCOM approval

#### Learn More

Designated "previously assigned in submarines." Assigned only to those personnel disqualified for submarine duty and serving in a submarine support billet. Detailed by the submarine community, but not reassigned to submarine duty without prior approval from NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403). (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 2)

Who assigns Designation 5 (SQ) to enlisted personnel?

#### Learn More

Submarine "designation 5 (SQ)" will be assigned by NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403) to enlisted personnel in submarine source ratings who are qualified in submarines, but not expected to serve again in the submarine force. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 3)

Are personnel with Designation 5 (SQ) entitled to wear the enlisted submarine breast insignia?

#### Learn More

Personnel assigned "designation 5 (SQ)" designator are entitled to wear the enlisted submarine breast insignia. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 3)

What permanent entry must be made in the ESR for personnel assigned Designation 5 (SQ)?

Assigned enlisted submarine Designation 5 (SQ), entitled to wear the enlisted submarine breast insignia, with authority cited

#### Learn More

Personnel assigned "designation 5 (SQ)" must have the following permanent NAVPERS 1070/613 Administrative Remarks entry made and uploaded to their electronic service record (ESR) via Navy Standard Integrated Personnel System (NSIPS). "(Date): Assigned enlisted submarine "Designation 5 (SQ)." Member entitled to wear the enlisted submarine breast insignia. Authority: (Cite the source document notifying of "Designation 5 (SQ))." (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 3)

Where must personnel with Designation 5 (SQ) or 7 (SG) request reinstatement to submarine duty?

MILPERSMAN 1306-416

#### Learn More

Personnel assigned "designation 5 (SQ)" or "designation 7 (SG)" who desire to return to submarine duty must formally request reinstatement using the procedures in MILPERSMAN 1306-416. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 3)

For what reasons is Designation 7 (SG) assigned by NAVPERSCOM?

Conversion to a nonsubmarine source rating or NEC detailed outside the submarine community, or approval of nonsubmarine source rating to be designated qualified in submarines

#### Learn More

Submarine "designation 7 (SG)" will be assigned by NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403) for the following reasons: (1) Conversion to a nonsubmarine source rating or to an NEC detailed outside of the submarine community. (2) Approval of nonsubmarine source rating to be designated "qualified in submarines." (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 3)

What permanent entry must be made in the ESR for personnel assigned Designation 7 (SG)?

Assigned enlisted submarine Designation 7 (SG), entitled to wear the enlisted submarine breast insignia, with authority cited

#### Learn More

Personnel assigned "designation 7 (SG)" designator must have the following permanent NAVPERS 1070/613 entry made in their ESR via NSIPS. "Date: Assigned enlisted submarine "designation 7 (SG)." Member entitled to wear the enlisted submarine breast insignia. Authority: (Cite the source document notifying of "designation 7 (SG))." (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 3)

Who may be assigned Designation 8 (SP) by NAVPERSCOM?

Personnel disqualified from submarine duty, normally assigned to a submarine support billet for a 2-year tour if a valid billet exists

Submarine "designation 8 (SP)" may be assigned by NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403) to personnel disqualified from submarine duty. Each person disqualified is normally assigned to a submarine support billet for a 2-year tour of duty if a valid billet exists. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 4)

What happens to submarine nuclear trained personnel physically disqualified from submarine duty?

Assigned as a surface nuclear operator, no formal Designation 8 (SP) notification or service record entries required

Submarine nuclear trained personnel who are physically disqualified from submarine duty will be assigned as a surface nuclear operator. No formal notification of "designation 8 (SP)" assignment is made by NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403) and service record entries are not required. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 4)

What is the purpose of assigning Designation 8 (SP)?

For NAVPERSCOM assignment and tracking, reflected on command enlisted distribution verification process (EDVP)

Assignment of "designation 8 (SP)" is for NAVPERSCOM assignment and tracking purposes only, but will reflect on a command enlisted distribution verification process (EDVP). (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 4)

What does the submarine qualification insignia represent?

Completion of prescribed training and successful qualification for submarine service

#### Learn More

The submarine qualification insignia represents the completion of prescribed training and the successful qualification for submarine service. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 4)

Does the submarine qualification insignia indicate current service in the submarine force?

No, it does not necessarily indicate current service in the submarine force

The insignia shows that the wearer has qualified for submarine duty, but does not necessarily indicate that the wearer is currently serving in the submarine force. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 4)

What happens to the submarine insignia privilege for enlisted members disqualified from submarine duty?

Normally revoked, but those physically disqualified may continue to wear it, or other unique circumstances may allow retention

Enlisted members who have been "disqualified" from submarine duty must normally, by the same authority, have their privilege to wear the submarine insignia revoked. A member who has been physically disqualified for submarine duty may continue to wear the submarine insignia. Other unique circumstances may convince the submarine disqualification authority to leave intact a former submariner's privilege to wear the submarine qualification insignia. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 4)

How must submarine designations SS or SU be used in a member's rate or rating abbreviation?

Placed in parenthesis immediately after (e.g., MM2 (SS), SA(SU))

Submarine designations "SS" or "SU" must be placed in parenthesis immediately after the member's rate or rating abbreviation (e.g., MM2 (SS), SA(SU)). (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 4)

Must submarine designations be included in service record pages and correspondence?

Yes, until the designator is revoked

All service record pages and all correspondence pertaining to the member thereafter must include the appropriate designation until such time as the submarine designator may be revoked. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 4)

Does a member retain their submarine designator after transfer from submarine duty?

Yes, unless removed or changed due to disqualification

#### Learn More

A member who has received a submarine designator and is subsequently transferred from duty in submarines will normally retain the designator, unless removed or changed due to disqualification. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 5)

Does entitlement to wear the enlisted submarine warfare insignia continue after transfer from submarine duty?

Yes, until revoked

#### Learn More

Additionally, entitlement to wear the enlisted submarine warfare insignia remains until such time that entitlement is revoked. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 5)

What permanent ESR entry must be made when a member is qualified in submarines?

Qualified in submarines, assigned Designation 1 (SS)

#### Learn More

When a member has been examined and is qualified for submarine duty, the following permanent NAVPERS 1070/613 entry must be made and uploaded to the ESR via NSIPS. Ensure this entry is permanent. "(Date): Qualified in submarines this date and assigned enlisted submarine "designation 1 (SS)." (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 5)

Who has authority to remove submarine designations?

NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403) and submarine force commanders

#### Learn More

Only NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403) and submarine force commanders have authority to remove submarine designations. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 5)

Who can submarine force commanders act on for removing designations?

Personnel under their operational or administrative command

#### Learn More

Submarine force commanders will act only on the cases of those personnel who are under their operational or administrative command. NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403) will act on all other cases. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 5)

Who has authority to assign enlisted submarine designations, except for Designation 1 (SS) for those completing qualifications?

#### Learn More

Authority for assignment of any enlisted submarine designation is NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403), with exception of the assignment of "designation 1 (SS)" to those "designation 2 (SU)" personnel who complete requirements for "qualification in submarines." (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 5)

Who can assign Designation 1 (SS) to personnel completing submarine qualification requirements?

Submarine force commanders

#### Learn More

The authority for this designation change is granted to the submarine force commanders. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 5)

What type of preservice drug abuse waivers are screened by OPNAV (N133D) for Nuclear Field Program entry?

Preservice experimental marijuana use

#### Learn More

Preservice drug abuse waivers by personnel requesting entry into the Nuclear Field Program will be screened by the office of the Chief of Naval Operations (OPNAV), Nuclear Enlisted Program (N133D). Waivers may be granted for preservice experimental marijuana use only. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 5)

Who screens preservice marijuana use waivers for personnel in the Delayed Entry Program requesting initial submarine training?

Commander, Navy Recruiting Command

Commander, Navy Recruiting Command - for personnel in administrative chain of command specifically outlined in reference (b). (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 6)

Is preservice experimental marijuana use by personnel in the Delayed Entry Program waiverable for submarine training?

Admission of preservice experimental use of marijuana by personnel in Delayed Entry Program is not waiverable. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 6)

Who screens preservice marijuana use waivers for personnel not in the Delayed Entry Program requesting initial submarine training?

NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403) - for personnel in all other commands. Waivers may be granted for preservice experimental marijuana use only. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 6)

What permanent ESR entry is required when a preservice marijuana use waiver is granted for submarine duty?

Waiver granted, future drug abuse results in permanent disqualification and separation, signed by member and witness

Any time a waiver is granted for preservice drug abuse, the following permanent NAVPERS 1070/613 entry will be uploaded into the ESR via NSIPS. "(Date): A preservice marijuana use waiver for submarine duty has been granted by (cite authority). I understand any future drug abuse will result in permanent submarine disqualification and processing for separation from naval service. MEMBER WITNESS (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 6)

Are drug abuse waivers granted for in-service drug abuse for initial submarine training?

Drug abuse waivers will not be granted for personnel with a history of in-service drug abuse desiring initial submarine training or for in-service drug abuse which occurs after commencement of initial submarine or nuclear field training. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 6)

Are personnel disqualified for drug abuse eligible for reinstatement to submarine duty?

Personnel disqualified from submarine duty for drug abuse are not eligible for reinstatement at any time. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 6)

How long do personnel disqualified for alcohol dependency have to request reinstatement to submarine duty?

Within 3 years of disqualification

Personnel disqualified for alcohol dependency may request reinstatement to submarine duty, per reference (c), within 3 years of the disqualification date. After 3 years, reinstatement will not be considered. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 6)

Can a commanding officer recommend disqualification for submarine duty as a substitute for disciplinary action?

A CO may, at any time, recommend to the appropriate disqualification authority, via the administrative chain of command, that any enlisted member serving under the CO’s command be declared disqualified for submarine duty for any reason other than as a substitute for appropriate disciplinary action in the case of disciplinary offenses. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 6)

Should disqualification be used instead of recommending separation when separation is warranted?

Disqualification must not be used in lieu of a recommendation for separation of a member from naval service when separation is warranted and appropriate. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 6)

When is a member undergoing separation proceedings disqualified from submarines?

When separation has been directed

#### Learn More

However, a member undergoing administrative or punitive separation proceedings will be disqualified from submarines when such separation has been directed. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 7)

What are the two general categories for removing a submarine designator?

Physical disqualification and all other reasons

#### Learn More

There are two general categories of justification for removal of a submarine designator: a. Physical disqualification; and b. All other reasons; (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 7)

What reasons may fall under "all other reasons" for removing a submarine designator?

Inability or refusal to qualify, substance abuse, loss of security clearance, removal from PRP, conscientious objector status, emotional instability

#### Learn More

All other reasons; this category may include, but is not limited to: (1) Inability or refusal to qualify or requalify in submarines; (2) Substance abuse (alcohol or drugs); (3) Loss of or inability to acquire a required security clearance, NEC, or limited access authority; (4) Removal from the Personnel Reliability Program (PRP); (5) Conscientious objector status; or (6) Emotional instability. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 7)

What permanent ESR entry is required for personnel physically disqualified from submarine duty?

Not physically qualified, designator removed, entitled to wear insignia if applicable, may reapply when physically qualified

#### Learn More

For personnel who are physically disqualified, the following permanent NAVPERS 1070/613 entry must be made and uploaded to ESR via NSIPS. "(Date): Determined "not physically qualified" for submarine duty for reasons stated in medical record. The enlisted submarine "designator (SS)/(SU)" is hereby removed. (Member's last name) is entitled to wear the submarine breast insignia (if applicable). (Member's last name) may reapply to NAVPERSCOM (PERS-403) for restoration of the (SS)/(SU) designator when member once again satisfies the physical requirements of the Manual of the Medical Department for submarine duty. Authority: (Cite the source document approving disqualification.)" (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 7)

What permanent ESR entry is required for personnel disqualified for reasons other than physical?

Disqualified for stated reason, designator removed, not eligible for return, not entitled to wear insignia if deemed appropriate

#### Learn More

For personnel who are disqualified for reasons other than physical, the following permanent NAVPERS 1070/613 entry must be made and uploaded to ESR via NSIPS. "(Date): Disqualified for submarine duty by reason of (state reason). The enlisted submarine "designator (SS)/(SU)" is hereby removed. (Member's last name) is not eligible for return to submarine duty at any future date and is not entitled to wear the submarine breast insignia (if deemed appropriate by the submarine disqualification authority). Authority: (Cite the source document approving disqualification)." (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 8)

Where are procedures for reinstatement to submarine duty after disqualification found?

Are personnel disqualified for drug abuse eligible for reinstatement per reference (c)?

Per reference c, personnel disqualified from submarine duty for drug abuse are ineligible for reinstatement. (MILPERSMAN 1220-040 - Submarine Qualifications Designation for Enlisted Members - Page 8)

How long do personnel disqualified for alcohol dependency have to request reinstatement per reference (c)?

Within 3 years of disqualification
